My approach

Four pillars of lasting weight loss

No extremes. Just the fundamentals, done consistently.

🥗

Balanced nutrition

Enjoy foods you like, in portions that work, with a calorie target based on your body and activity.

🚶

Daily movement

Start where you are. We build activity you enjoy, from walking to strength training, at a pace that suits you.

😴

Sleep & stress

Rest and stress directly affect hunger and cravings, so we address them alongside food and exercise.

🧠

Mindset & habits

Small, repeatable habits and a kinder relationship with food make the results stick.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No. We focus on balanced, flexible eating built around foods you enjoy, so you can follow it for life rather than for a few weeks.

How fast will I lose weight?

A steady pace of roughly 0.25–0.5 kg (about ½–1 lb) per week is a healthy, sustainable range for most people. Individual results vary.

Do I need to exercise a lot?

Not at all. We start with what's realistic for you, often just walking more, and build gradually.

Is coaching a substitute for medical advice?

No. Coaching complements, but doesn't replace, care from your doctor. If you have a medical condition or take medication, please check with your doctor before changing your diet or exercise.

How do sessions take place?

Sessions and check-ins are held online, so you can join from anywhere that suits you.
